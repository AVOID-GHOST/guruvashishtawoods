Our Story & Vision

Guru Vashishta Woods Pvt. Ltd is a renowned home and office furniture company, specializing in handmade crafts and fully customized solutions. Located in the heart of Jaipur, Rajasthan, we bring the rich heritage of Indian craftsmanship to modern luxury living.

We work tirelessly to provide handmade, customized furniture tailored to the specific requirements of your home or office space. Our vision is to create and design world-class furniture by working artistically, reducing costs, and continually improving our services.

We justify our business by adding tremendous value to our designs for our esteemed customers, ensuring that every piece of furniture we craft tells a story of luxury, durability, and timeless elegance.
